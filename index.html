Quick installation guide

Secure your race number.

Four simple steps to attach your race number using Pish Tags magnetic fasteners.

⚠ Medical devices: read first

  • Do not use Pish Tags if you have a pacemaker, ICD (implantable defibrillator) or any other implanted medical device. Strong magnets worn on the chest can interfere with these devices.
1

Separate the magnets

Slide each matching pair apart sideways. Don't try to pull them straight apart. Keep the pieces well apart so they don't snap back together, and keep each pair together so you can use them one at a time.

2

Position your race number

Put your jersey on a flat surface, or put it on first. Place your race number on the outside of the jersey where you want it to sit. Cycling? If your number goes on your back, ask someone to help you fit it, or fit it before you put the jersey on.

3

Attach the magnets

Hold one magnet inside the jersey, behind a corner of the race number. Bring its matching magnet to the front, over the same spot, and let it close slowly. The race number and jersey sit between the pair. Keep your fingertips clear of the gap.

4

Repeat and check

Fit a pair at each of the four corners, a little in from the edge of the number. Give each corner a gentle tug and check the number lies flat. Check again before the start.

Safety

  • Keep away from children and pets. These are strong magnets. If anyone swallows a magnet, especially more than one, get medical help immediately. Swallowed magnets can attract each other inside the body and cause serious injury.
  • Pinch hazard: magnet pairs snap together forcefully. Keep fingers and skin out of the gap.
  • Don't let magnets snap together hard. They can chip or crack. Stop using any magnet that is chipped or cracked.
  • Keep magnets away from bank cards with magnetic strips, hotel key cards and mechanical watches.
  • Check all four fasteners before every event.

Care & storage

  • Remove the magnets before washing your jersey.
  • After a sweaty or wet event, wipe the magnets clean and let them dry before storing.
  • To remove, slide each front magnet off sideways rather than pulling the race number.
  • Store the magnets in pairs in their container when not in use.
