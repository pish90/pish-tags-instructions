Quick installation guide

Secure your race number.

Four simple steps to attach your race number using Pish Tags magnetic fasteners.

1

Separate the magnets

Gently pull each matching pair apart. Keep the pairs together so you can use them one at a time.

2

Position your race number

Place your race number on the outside of your jersey where you want it to sit.

3

Attach the magnets

Place one magnet on the front and its matching magnet directly behind the fabric. The race number and jersey sit between the pair.

4

Repeat and check

Use all four magnet pairs. Check that each is firmly attached and your race number is secure before you start.

Care & safety

• Keep magnets away from small children; they can be dangerous if swallowed.

• Keep magnets away from devices or medical implants that may be affected by magnets. Follow the device or implant manufacturer's guidance.

• Check all four fasteners before every event. Store the magnets together in their container when not in use.

• Avoid pulling the race number sharply; separate the magnets gently when removing them.
